    Optional ExperimentalmaxSteps

    maxSteps?: number

    Largest number of steps one event may ask for. Default: the platform's 0.5–3.0 zoom range expressed in units of the effective ContentZoomWheelReaderOptions.zoomStep (25 at the default step of 0.1) — so overriding zoomStep scales this default with it.

    This property is unstable and may change shape or disappear without notice

    Optional Experimentalwindow

    window?: Window

    Window the modifier listeners attach to. Default globalThis.window; tests pass jsdom's. When neither exists (a reader created outside a DOM, such as under Node) the reader still reads notch and pinch counts from the events it is handed — it just cannot tell a synthesized pinch from a real one held down by a physically-pressed Ctrl or Cmd key.

    Optional ExperimentalzoomStep

    zoomStep?: number

    Zoom step the pinch calibration is derived from. Default 0.1. Must be a positive, finite number; the reader does not validate it, and a zero or negative step yields a meaningless calibration.

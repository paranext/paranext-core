      Validates a tab menu and the anchor it opens at: the menu's shape (columns, groups and items as the tab menu reads them), its item count and label lengths, that no submenu contains itself, and that the anchor is a rectangle of finite numbers. The menu comes from a WebView but renders in the window, so anything here that would throw while rendering must be refused up front.

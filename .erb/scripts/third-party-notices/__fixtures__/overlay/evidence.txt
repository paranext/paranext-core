Standing in for the packaging file a real overlay's delivery evidence points at.

either is gone - so a reviewed determination cannot outlive the mechanism that put the program in
the installer. The fixture overlay beside this file points its two deliveries here rather than at
`electron-builder.json5`, so that editing the real packaging config cannot break a test about
merging, and so that the substrings below say what they are for.
